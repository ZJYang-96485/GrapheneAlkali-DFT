**Figure 6. Adsorption energies and available adsorbate-specific cutoff validation.**
(a) Li/C50 and (b) Na/C50 adsorption energies are compared between the production 65/520 Ry cutoff pair and a 70/560 Ry fixed-geometry check, with the wavefunction and charge-density cutoffs written as `ecutwfc/ecutrho`. Raising the cutoff changes $E_\mathrm{ads}$ by −2.10 meV for Li/C50 and −10.59 meV for Na/C50. Panels (c) and (d) retain the accepted 65/520 Ry vacancy-adsorption energies of −2.71741 eV for Li/C49 and −2.01581 eV for Na/C49, while explicitly marking the unavailable 70/560 Ry values as not calculated. Adsorption energies use $E_\mathrm{ads}=E(\mathrm{M/C_n})-E(\mathrm{C_n})-E(\mathrm{M_atom})$; more negative values indicate stronger exothermic adsorption. The points are deterministic DFT results, so no statistical error bars are shown. The pristine cutoff test supports numerical stability for Li/C50 and Na/C50 but cannot establish vacancy-specific cutoff convergence or ionic-relaxation convergence at 70/560 Ry.
