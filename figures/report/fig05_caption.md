**Figure 5. Numerical convergence of pristine C50 graphene.**
Each vertical axis shows the total-energy difference per carbon atom, in meV/C, relative to the most demanding point of the corresponding sweep: 70/560 Ry in (a), 12×12×1 k points in (b), and 0.010 Ry smearing in (c). The dashed line in panels (a) and (b) marks the predefined 1 meV/C convergence criterion; the smearing panel retains signed differences on a much narrower scale, so its ±1 meV/C limits lie outside the displayed range. The selected 65/520 Ry cutoff differs from 70/560 Ry by 0.9769 meV/C, while 6×6×1 and 9×9×1 differ from 12×12×1 by 0.1375 and 0.0183 meV/C and are used for relaxations and final SCF calculations, respectively. Changing the Marzari–Vanderbilt smearing from the selected 0.010 Ry to 0.005 or 0.020 Ry shifts the energy by only +0.0496 or −0.0536 meV/C. These deterministic convergence differences do not have statistical error bars.
