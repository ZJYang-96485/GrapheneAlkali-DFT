**Figure S2. Available two-point numerical checks for vacancy-containing systems.** (a) The total-energy difference per carbon atom for the same fixed Li/C49 geometry at 55/440 and 65/520 Ry, both sampled with a 6 x 6 x 1 k-point mesh and 0.01 Ry smearing. The 55/440 Ry result is 2.736 meV/C above the 65/520 Ry reference and therefore does not meet the predefined 1 meV/C energy criterion; nevertheless, the largest force-component change is only 1.4079 x 10^-4 Ry/Bohr (below the 4 x 10^-4 Ry/Bohr pilot tolerance) and the total magnetization is unchanged. (b) Signed total-energy changes after increasing the k-point mesh from 6 x 6 x 1 at the final relaxation step to a fixed-geometry 9 x 9 x 1 final SCF at 65/520 Ry. The changes are -0.020, +0.026, and +0.014 meV/C for C49, Li/C49, and Na/C49, respectively, showing negligible k-point sensitivity of the final total energies on this scale. Every plotted calculation used GPU acceleration, achieved electronic convergence, and printed `JOB DONE`. These are two-point checks rather than complete vacancy cutoff, k-point, or smearing sweeps; no vacancy smearing sweep or 70/560 Ry vacancy adsorption-energy calculation was performed.
