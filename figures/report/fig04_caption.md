**Figure 4. Relaxed adsorption height and magnetic response of Li and Na on pristine and monovacancy graphene.**
(a) The adsorbate height is the perpendicular distance from the metal atom to the mean carbon plane, in Å. Li remains closer to graphene than Na for both substrates: 1.69 versus 2.25 Å on pristine C50 and 1.80 versus 2.27 Å on monovacancy C49. (b) The total magnetization is reported in Bohr magnetons per cell; pristine Li/C50 and Na/C50 are nearly nonmagnetic at 0.00 and 0.13 μB/cell, whereas Li/C49 and Na/C49 retain moments of 0.96 and 1.00 μB/cell. The dashed segment marks the bare-C49 value of 1.60 μB/cell, showing that adsorption reduces but does not eliminate the vacancy-associated moment. All quantities come from the accepted 65/520 Ry, 6×6×1 ionic relaxations rather than the 9×9×1 final-SCF energy calculations; statistical error bars are not shown because no ensemble uncertainty estimate is available.
