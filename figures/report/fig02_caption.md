**Figure 2. Adsorption energies of Li and Na on pristine and monovacancy graphene.**
The vertical axis shows the DFT adsorption energy, \(E_\mathrm{ads}\), in eV; more-negative values indicate stronger adsorption. Colored bars are the present PBE-D3(BJ) results from the internally consistent 65/520 Ry, 9×9×1 final-SCF dataset, while open diamonds are published comparison values (Olsson *et al.* for pristine graphene and Cottom *et al.* for monovacancy graphene). The monovacancy strengthens adsorption relative to pristine graphene by 1.55 eV for Li and 1.45 eV for Na, and Li binds more strongly than Na on both substrates. Differences from the vacancy literature values are interpreted as method- and cell-size-sensitive because the published study used a different code, basis, supercell, and sampling; statistical error bars are not shown because these are deterministic DFT calculations and no common uncertainty estimate is available.
