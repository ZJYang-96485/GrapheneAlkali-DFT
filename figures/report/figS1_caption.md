**Figure S1. Residual-force convergence of the accepted ionic relaxations.**
The bars show the largest absolute Cartesian force component remaining in each accepted 65/520 Ry, 6×6×1 relaxation, in Ry/Bohr, on a logarithmic vertical scale. The dashed line marks the common `forc_conv_thr` criterion of $4.0\times10^{-4}$ Ry/Bohr. Every system lies below the threshold: pristine C50 has the smallest residual at $2.07\times10^{-6}$ Ry/Bohr, while Na/C49 is closest to the limit at $3.9186\times10^{-4}$ Ry/Bohr, or 97.97% of the threshold. These deterministic convergence diagnostics do not have statistical error bars and should not be interpreted as physical force uncertainties.
