**Figure 3. Defect enhancement and metal selectivity in alkali adsorption on graphene.**
(a) The vacancy-strengthening magnitude is defined as \(\Delta E_\mathrm{vac}=E_\mathrm{ads}(\mathrm{C50})-E_\mathrm{ads}(\mathrm{C49})\), so a positive value means that adsorption is more exothermic at the monovacancy. (b) The Li–Na selectivity is defined as \(\Delta E_\mathrm{Li-Na}=E_\mathrm{ads}(\mathrm{Na})-E_\mathrm{ads}(\mathrm{Li})\), so a positive value means that Li binds more strongly. Using the internally consistent 65/520 Ry, 9×9×1 final-SCF dataset, the vacancy strengthens adsorption by 1.55 eV for Li and 1.45 eV for Na, while Li binds more strongly than Na by 0.60 eV on pristine graphene and 0.70 eV at the monovacancy. Statistical error bars are not shown because the values are deterministic energy differences and no ensemble uncertainty estimate is available.
